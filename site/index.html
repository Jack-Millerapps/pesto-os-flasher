Pesto Player

Flash your SD card from a Chromebook

Download the Pesto Player OS image, then write it to an SD card with Chromebook Recovery Utility. No terminal and no Raspberry Pi Imager.

Heads up: a web page is not allowed to write to an SD card (browsers block that). This page downloads the image and walks you through the flash. The actual writing is done by Chromebook Recovery Utility.

2. Flash it

You need an SD card of 16 GB or more (a name-brand card is worth it) and a USB or built-in SD reader. Everything on the card will be erased.

  1. Install Chromebook Recovery Utility (free, from Google).
    Open in Chrome Web Store
  2. Download the image above. Leave it as the .img.zip file. Do not unzip it.
  3. Insert the SD card into your Chromebook.
  4. Open Chromebook Recovery Utility from the launcher and click Get started.
  5. Click the gear icon in the top-right corner, then choose Use local image.
  6. Pick the pesto-os-...img.zip file you downloaded (it is in your Downloads folder).
  7. Choose your SD card from the drop-down. Check the size so you do not pick the wrong drive.
  8. Click Continue, then Create now. Wait for it to write and verify; it can take 10 minutes or more. Do not remove the card until it says it is done.
  9. Optional but recommended: add your Wi-Fi (step 3 below) before putting the card in the Pi.
  10. Put the card in the Pi, plug in power, and wait. See what to expect.

3. Wi-Fi helper (optional)

Fill this in to make a pesto-wifi.txt file. After flashing, open the card in your Files app (the drive called bootfs / boot) and copy the file onto it. The Pi reads it on boot and joins your network.

Private: this form works entirely in your browser. Your network name and password are never sent anywhere; the file is created on your device.

8 to 63 characters. Leave empty only for an open network.

The Pi supports 2.4 GHz networks (the Zero 2 W has no 5 GHz radio).

4. First boot

  • The first boot takes a few minutes. It sets itself up and may restart once. Be patient and keep power connected.
  • The screen will come up with Pesto Player when it is done.
  • The default hostname is pesto, so on your network it is reachable as pesto.local.
  • Add music from the player: Library, then Add music, then scan the QR code with your phone.

Troubleshooting

My SD card is not listed in Recovery Utility

Remove and re-insert the card, then close and reopen the app. Make sure the card is at least 16 GB and the reader is fully seated. Try a different reader or USB port. Some very new or very large cards (above 128 GB) can be fussy; a 16 to 64 GB card is the safe choice.

Recovery Utility shows an error

Re-download the image and check the SHA256 matches (step 1). Pick the .img.zip as-is; do not unzip it. Make sure you have enough free space in Downloads and that the download finished. If it fails partway, try another card, since a worn or fake card is the usual cause.

The Pi does not connect to Wi-Fi

Check that pesto-wifi.txt is in the top level of the boot drive (not in a folder) and that the name and password are exact, with correct capitals. The network must be 2.4 GHz. You can also connect a keyboard and fix it from the player's settings.

The screen stays black after several minutes

Wait up to 5 minutes on the very first boot. Check the power supply (a weak charger causes odd behaviour), that the display is seated on the GPIO header, and re-flash the card if it still does not start.
